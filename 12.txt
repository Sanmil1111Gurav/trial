Aim: To study and test message integrity by using MD5, SHA-1 for varying message sizes.

        md5 example.txt

--> MD5:
        md5 example.txt

--> SHA:
        sha1sum example.txt

echo This is the demo of sha > example.txt
cat example.txt

--> SHA:
        sha1sum example.txt
        sha224sum example.txt
        sha256sum example.txt
        sha384sum example.txt
        sha512sum example.txt


Conclusion : 
We have seen how checksum are generated for MD5 and SHA. 
You can make use of this Checksum method as a redundancy check to detect errors in data. 
Hence. ensure the integrity of data portions for data transmission or storage.
